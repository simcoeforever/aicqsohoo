Authorization and public records

Only the wallet owner may authorize payment. An AI needs explicit owner permission; visiting or connecting does not authorize a transfer. The EIP-712 signature authorizes this test-token transfer, not a harmless login.

Blockchain addresses and transfers are public. In a separate private payment ledger we store a payment ID, fixed terms, UTC date, status and transaction hash; no raw signature/payment payload, payer address, IP, UA or visitor identifier is logged by our application. The browser session retains only the payment ID to retrieve the receipt. Wallets and infrastructure providers have their own data processing. No payment log or transaction list is published.

The trial is limited to 5 attempts per UTC day and 20 in total, including unsigned starts. Records are retained through this bounded experiment; deleting/resetting IDs will not reopen old authorizations. Anyone can exhaust the small quota. Duplicate IDs return the existing result; uncertain settlement stops for reconciliation. Do not sign/pay again with a new ID when a result is pending.
